# Domain Glossary

- **Evidence Observation** — An immutable physical source record, including its native identity, original payload, observed time, and provenance.
- **Business Event** — A business-effective occurrence in an order lifecycle, distinct from when evidence of it was observed.
- **Canonical Order** — The channel-namespaced commercial order whose identity and attributes reconcile all relevant evidence observations.
- **Settlement Record** — A seller-perspective account of signed collection, refund or reversal, fee, and payout amounts for an order.
- **Canonical Exception** — A named, traceable condition where evidence cannot create a valid fact or values do not reconcile under the contract.
- **Lineage Edge** — A relationship explaining how one evidence observation contributes to, corroborates, or does not contribute to a canonical fact.
